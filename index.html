About Us

Rebel Robotics Booster Club, Inc. is a New York nonprofit organization organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes. We support Rebel Robotics (FIRST® Robotics Competition Team 2638) at Great Neck South High School through fundraising, volunteer support, equipment purchases, competition travel, educational opportunities, and community partnerships.

Since 2007, Rebel Robotics has provided students with opportunities to develop engineering, programming, leadership, teamwork, and problem-solving skills through the FIRST Robotics Competition.

Our Mission

STEM Education

Support hands-on engineering, programming, and robotics education.

Student Opportunity

Provide funding and logistical support for competitions and team activities.

Mentorship

Connect students with mentors, alumni, and STEM professionals.

Community

Expand access to STEM through outreach and community engagement.
