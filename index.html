About the Booster Club

Supporting students, mentors, families, and the long-term strength of Team 2638.

Rebel Robotics Booster Club, Inc. is a nonprofit organization organized under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. We support Rebel Robotics, FIRST Robotics Competition Team 2638, at Great Neck South High School by helping fund competitions, equipment, fabrication materials, travel, student training, outreach, mentorship, and team-building activities.

Founded in 2007, Rebel Robotics began competing as FIRST Robotics Competition Team 2638 in 2008. Today, the team operates like a varsity team, engineering lab, and leadership incubator, open to students ready to learn, build, and lead.

2026 Season Highlights

What a season for Team 2638.

Rebel Robotics earned Excellence in Engineering at the FIRST Long Island Regional and again at the Rocket City Regional in Huntsville, Alabama, where the team also finished as Regional Finalists.

#112 in the 2026 Regional Championship Pool, placing Team 2638 in the top 3% of FRC teams worldwide.
    Booster Impact

    A first full year of parent-led booster support.

    The 2026 season was the first full year of Rebel Robotics Booster Club, Inc., a parent-led 501(c)(3) supporting Team 2638's 120+ students. The Booster Club welcomed five new corporate sponsors, organized the Friends and Family Motch Match fundraiser, and supported team lunches and logistics during competition weekends.

    Parent, alumni, mentor, and company networks help FRC programs stay funded and give students the resources to keep building, traveling, competing, and serving the community.

    Board & Governance

    Volunteer-led and mission-focused.

    Rebel Robotics Booster Club, Inc. is governed by a volunteer Board of Directors dedicated to supporting students, mentors, families, and the long-term success of Rebel Robotics.

    The Booster Club supports charitable and educational purposes, maintains board oversight of finances and operations, follows conflict-of-interest procedures, and supports Rebel Robotics students through fundraising, logistics, mentorship, outreach, and educational programming.
